# Product vision

> Fill in and keep to one page. Revisit when priorities change.

## For whom

For people that want to be a bit anonymous but still part of a safe and secure community to be able to discuss things they can not discuss with their ordinary friends.

## Problem

Today they are driven to strange sites with strange people or they are left alone.

## Outcome

They become part of a nice and warm community, where the community helps foster bad behaviour.

## Product in one sentence

For singles and couples that want to meet likeminded open people in a safe and secure way

## Business model

- Free tier: Personal page, chat, limited bandwith on text and photos, limited overview of visitors
- Subscription: Additional bandwith on text and photos, overview of visitors
- Ads: No 3rd part ads

## Principles

- Security and privacy before features.
- Start small, ship slices users can try, learn, then grow.
- Low running costs; no paid service without a clear need.
- Interfaces should be visually appealing, simple to use, and work well on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
